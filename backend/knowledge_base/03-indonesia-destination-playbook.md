# Indonesia Destination Playbook

KelanaAI's house guidance for planning Indonesia. Seasons, routing and the
operational rules our designers follow.

> Entry requirements, fees and health rules change without notice and are set by
> the Indonesian government, not by KelanaAI. Nothing in this document is
> immigration advice. Always send travellers to the official Directorate General
> of Immigration site and their own embassy before they book.

## 1. When to go

| Window | Conditions | KelanaAI guidance |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| May to September | Dry season, busiest | Best weather, highest prices. Book Bali stays 10-12 weeks ahead. |
| July and August | Peak of peak | Add 25-40% to the accommodation slice. Avoid promising Standard-tier beachfront. |
| October and November | Shoulder, short afternoon rain | Best value window. Our default recommendation for Standard budgets. |
| December to March | Wet season | Rain most afternoons. Fine for culture and cities, poor for diving and volcano sunrises. |
| April | Shoulder, drying out | Good value, low crowds. |

Nyepi, the Balinese day of silence, closes the whole island for 24 hours -
the airport shuts, nobody leaves their accommodation, and lights must stay off
after dark. Never schedule an arrival, departure or activity on Nyepi or the day
before. Check the date each year; it moves.

## 2. Routing rules

**Bali.** Plan by zone, not by island. Moving between zones eats a half-day.

| Zone | Character | Good for |
|------|-----------|----------|
| South (Seminyak, Canggu, Uluwatu) | Beach clubs, surf, traffic | Nightlife, surfing, first-timers |
| Ubud and central | Rice terraces, temples, art | Culture, wellness, families |
| East (Amed, Sidemen) | Quiet, volcanic, diving | Diving, slow travel |
| North (Lovina) | Remote, black sand | Dolphins, hot springs |
| Nusa islands | Cliffs, snorkelling | Day trips and 2-3 night add-ons |

Do not plan more than **two** Bali zones in a 5-day trip. South-to-Ubud is 1.5 to
2.5 hours depending on traffic; treat any zone move as a lost half-day.

**Java.** Yogyakarta is the cultural anchor - Borobudur and Prambanan are both
day trips from it. Mount Bromo sunrise needs a 02:00 start from Probolinggo or
Malang, so budget a full recovery afternoon after it. Never stack Bromo and Ijen
on consecutive nights for Standard-tier travellers; it is two overnight starts
back to back.

**Komodo and Flores.** Access is through Labuan Bajo. Liveaboards run 2 to 4
nights. Sea conditions are poor from January to March; do not plan liveaboards
in that window.

## 3. Minimum trip lengths

KelanaAI will not sell these itineraries below the stated length, because they
cannot be delivered well:

| Itinerary | Minimum nights |
|-----------|----------------|
| Bali, single zone | 4 |
| Bali, two zones | 6 |
| Bali plus Nusa Penida | 7 |
| Yogyakarta with Borobudur and Prambanan | 3 |
| Yogyakarta plus Bromo | 5 |
| Komodo liveaboard from Labuan Bajo | 4 |
| Java overland, Jakarta to Bali | 12 |

If a traveller asks for less, the assistant offers the shorter version of the
route rather than compressing the full one.

## 4. Costs worth knowing

The Bali tourist levy is a one-off payment per foreign visitor on arrival, paid
online or at the airport. Confirm the current amount in the supplier feed before
quoting it - it is set by the provincial government and we do not control it.
Budget it as a fixed arrival cost, not a daily cost.

Temple entry, guides and the mandatory sarong rental are activity-slice costs.
Private drivers in Bali are booked by the day, not the hour, and a full day is
10 hours including the driver's waiting time.

## 5. Practical house rules

1. Always name the specific beach, temple or warung. "A local restaurant" is not
   an acceptable recommendation in a KelanaAI itinerary.
2. Flag scooter hire as requiring a valid international driving permit and a
   helmet. Do not recommend scooters to travellers who have not ridden before.
3. For volcano sunrises, state the wake-up time in the itinerary. Travellers who
   discover a 02:00 start on the day are our most common complaint.
4. Ramadan affects opening hours across Java and Lombok but barely affects Bali,
   which is majority Hindu. Plan Java trips around it, not Bali trips.
5. Sunday is the busiest day at Borobudur and Prambanan. Prefer a weekday.
