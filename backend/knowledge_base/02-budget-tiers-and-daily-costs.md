# KelanaAI Budget Tiers and Daily Cost Playbook

How KelanaAI turns a total budget into a category, and the internal per-day
planning allowances a designer should assume for each city.

> The city allowances below are **KelanaAI's internal planning figures**, agreed
> with our partner suppliers for 2026. They are what the planner budgets against.
> They are not live market prices - always confirm the current supplier quote
> before promising a number to a traveller.

## 1. How the category is decided

The trip category comes from the **total** trip budget, not the daily budget:

| Total budget (USD) | Category |
|--------------------|-----------|
| Under 1,000 | Backpacker |
| 1,000 to 3,000 inclusive | Standard |
| Over 3,000 | Luxury |

The daily budget is simply the total budget divided by the number of days. The
category never changes when the number of days changes - only the total budget
moves it. A 20-day trip on USD 900 is still a Backpacker trip at USD 45 a day.

When a traveller edits the budget on a saved trip, KelanaAI recalculates both the
category and the daily budget from the new total.

## 2. The comfort floor

A trip is flagged **below the comfort floor** when the daily budget falls under
the city's Backpacker allowance in the table below. The assistant should say so
plainly and offer two options: shorten the trip, or raise the budget. It must
never silently plan a trip that cannot be delivered.

The comfort floor is advisory. Travellers may proceed anyway, and the planner
will still produce an itinerary that leans on free sights, street food and
dormitory or guesthouse stays.

## 3. Internal per-day allowances, 2026

Figures are USD per person per day and include accommodation, food, local
transport and one paid activity. They exclude international flights and
insurance.

| City | Backpacker | Standard | Luxury |
|------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Bali, Indonesia | 35 | 95 | 260 |
| Yogyakarta, Indonesia | 25 | 70 | 180 |
| Bangkok, Thailand | 35 | 100 | 280 |
| Hanoi, Vietnam | 30 | 85 | 230 |
| Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia | 40 | 105 | 270 |
| Singapore | 75 | 190 | 480 |
| Kyoto, Japan | 85 | 210 | 520 |
| Tokyo, Japan | 90 | 225 | 560 |
| Seoul, South Korea | 70 | 175 | 430 |
| Lisbon, Portugal | 75 | 170 | 400 |
| Paris, France | 95 | 230 | 600 |
| Reykjavik, Iceland | 130 | 290 | 700 |
| Marrakech, Morocco | 40 | 110 | 300 |
| Dubai, UAE | 85 | 220 | 650 |

Cities not in this table default to the regional average: 40 Backpacker /
110 Standard / 290 Luxury for Southeast Asia, and 90 / 220 / 550 for Western
Europe and Japan.

## 4. How a daily budget should be split

The planner assumes this split when choosing recommendations:

| Component | Backpacker | Standard | Luxury |
|-----------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Accommodation | 40% | 45% | 50% |
| Food | 30% | 25% | 20% |
| Local transport | 15% | 10% | 8% |
| Activities | 15% | 20% | 22% |

For a Standard trip in Kyoto at 210 a day, that is roughly 95 for the room, 52
for food, 21 for transport and 42 for activities.

## 5. Rules the planner must follow

1. Never recommend a paid activity that costs more than the whole activity slice
   for that day, unless it is explicitly flagged as a splurge that replaces the
   next day's activity budget too.
2. On Backpacker trips, at least one meal per day must be a street-food or
   market option.
3. On Luxury trips, at least one experience per trip must be private or
   guided - a shared group tour does not satisfy the Luxury tier.
4. Group trips of 6 or more get a 12% per-person reduction on the accommodation
   slice, because rooms are shared. Apply it before choosing hotels.
5. Solo travellers in the Standard tier get a 10% uplift on accommodation,
   because single-occupancy rooms rarely cost half a double.

## 6. Currency

All KelanaAI budgets are stored and displayed in USD. The planner does not
convert to local currency in stored data. When a traveller gives a budget in
another currency, convert at the supplier feed's rate on the day, note the rate
used in the trip, and store the USD figure.
