# KelanaAI Service Policies

Internal operating rules for KelanaAI. This is the authoritative source for
booking, refund and support questions. Nothing here is public regulation - it
describes how KelanaAI itself operates.

## 1. Membership tiers

KelanaAI has three membership tiers. Tier is separate from the *trip category*
(Backpacker / Standard / Luxury) that the planner calculates from a budget.

| Tier | Price | Trips saved | AI itineraries per month | Support response |
|------|-------|-------------|--------------------------|------------------|
| Wanderer | Free | 10 | 5 | 48 hours, email only |
| Voyager | USD 9 / month | Unlimited | 60 | 12 hours, email and chat |
| Nomad | USD 29 / month | Unlimited | Unlimited | 2 hours, chat and phone |

Itinerary quota resets at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each calendar month.
Unused itineraries do not roll over. A regenerated itinerary for an existing
trip counts as a new itinerary against the quota.

Downgrading from Voyager or Nomad to Wanderer keeps every saved trip readable,
but the account cannot create a new trip until it is back under the 10-trip
limit. KelanaAI never deletes a trip on downgrade.

## 2. Booking changes and cancellations

KelanaAI does not sell flights or hotels directly. It books through partner
suppliers, so two policies always apply: the supplier's and ours. Where they
conflict, the supplier's cancellation window governs the refund, and KelanaAI's
service fee policy governs our own fee.

**KelanaAI service fee**

- The planning service fee is USD 25 per booked trip.
- The service fee is fully refundable if the booking is cancelled within
  **24 hours** of confirmation.
- After 24 hours the service fee is non-refundable, with one exception: if
  KelanaAI made a factual error in the itinerary that caused the cancellation,
  the fee is refunded in full and the trip is replanned at no charge.

**Refund timing**

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds take
5 to 10 business days. Bank transfer refunds take up to 15 business days. Refunds
are never issued as store credit unless the traveller asks for credit in writing.

## 3. Rebooking after a supplier cancellation

If a supplier cancels (airline schedule change, hotel overbooking), KelanaAI
rebooks the traveller at no service-fee cost. The rebooking rules are:

1. KelanaAI offers up to **three** alternatives within 4 hours of learning of the
   cancellation.
2. If any alternative costs less than the original, the difference is refunded.
3. If every alternative costs more, KelanaAI absorbs up to **USD 150** per
   traveller of the difference. Anything above that is quoted to the traveller
   before booking, never charged automatically.
4. If the traveller rejects all three alternatives, the booking is cancelled and
   the supplier's refund rules apply, but the KelanaAI service fee is refunded
   regardless of the 24-hour window.

## 4. Group trips

A trip is a **group trip** at 6 or more travellers.

- Group trips require a 30% deposit at booking; the balance is due 45 days before
  departure.
- The free cancellation window for group trips is 60 days before departure, not
  24 hours.
- One group organiser is named on the booking. Only the organiser can change or
  cancel it.
- Group trips are planned by a human travel designer, not only by the AI. The
  AI itinerary is a starting draft that a designer reviews before it is sent.

## 5. Travel disruption support

Nomad and Voyager members get disruption support: if a flight is delayed more
than 4 hours or cancelled while travelling, KelanaAI's support desk will
rearrange the remaining itinerary at no charge. Wanderer members receive a
regenerated itinerary but must rebook suppliers themselves.

Disruption support does not cover trip insurance claims. KelanaAI does not sell
or underwrite insurance and never advises on claim outcomes.

## 6. Data and privacy

- Trips are private to the account that created them. No other account, on any
  tier, can read them.
- Saved trips are retained for 24 months after the last time the account signs
  in, then deleted.
- Deleting a trip in the app removes it and its AI itinerary immediately and
  permanently. There is no undo and no admin recovery.
- Account deletion removes every trip within 30 days.

## 7. What the assistant must not do

The KelanaAI assistant must refuse and hand over to a human when asked to:

- confirm visa or immigration eligibility for a specific person,
- give medical, vaccination or legal advice,
- promise a refund outside the rules above,
- quote a price that is not in the current supplier feed.

For anything in the first two categories, the assistant points the traveller to
the relevant embassy, licensed clinic or lawyer.
